FAQ

  • Where is my progress stored? All game data is kept only on your device. No account or sign-in is required.
  • Do I need an internet connection? No, the game is fully playable offline.
  • Are there ads or paid content? The game has no ads and no subscriptions. It is free to play from the garage up to Series A; an optional one-time Full Version purchase unlocks the entire journey.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Be Unicorn does not collect, store, or share any personal data with third parties. The app uses no analytics, advertising networks, or tracking technologies.

Data

  • Your game progress (your company, your settings) is stored only on your device, inside the app's own sandbox. It is never sent to our servers or anywhere else.
  • If you delete the app, this data is removed from your device as well.
  • No internet connection is used; no data is transmitted over the network.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it is safe for all age groups, including children.
